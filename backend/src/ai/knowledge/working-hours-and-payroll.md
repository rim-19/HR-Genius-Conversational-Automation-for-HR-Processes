# Working Hours & Payroll — HR-Genius Technologies

## Working Hours
Standard working hours are **40 hours per week**, Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 with a one-hour lunch break. Core collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00. Flexible start times between 08:00 and 10:00 are permitted with manager agreement.

## Overtime
Overtime must be pre-approved by a manager. Approved overtime is compensated as time off in lieu at a rate of 1.25 hours per overtime hour worked.

## Payroll Schedule
Salaries are paid **monthly, on the 27th of each month**. If the 27th falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the last preceding working day. Payslips are issued through the HR-Genius platform.

## Salary Reviews
Formal salary reviews take place once per year, in January, and are based on performance and market benchmarks. Off-cycle adjustments require Human Resources Department and department-head approval.

## Expense Reimbursement
Work-related expenses are reimbursed within 15 working days of an approved claim. Claims must include a receipt and be submitted within 30 days of the expense.
