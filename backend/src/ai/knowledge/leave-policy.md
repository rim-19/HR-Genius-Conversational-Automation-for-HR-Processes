# Leave & Time-Off Policy — HR-Genius Technologies

## Annual Leave
Every full-time employee at HR-Genius Technologies is entitled to **20 paid annual leave days per calendar year**. Leave accrues monthly and unused days may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

Annual leave must be requested through the HR-Genius platform at least **7 calendar days in advance** for absences of 3 days or more. Requests are reviewed by the employee's direct manager and then confirmed by the Human Resources Department.

## Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to **12 paid sick days per year**. A medical certificate is required for any sick absence longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not count against the annual leave balance.

## Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave may be granted at management discretion for personal circumstances once annual leave is exhausted. Unpaid leave does not accrue benefits and must be approved by both the direct manager and HR.

## Public Holidays
HR-Genius Technologies observes all official public holidays in Morocco. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, no substitute day is granted.

## Parental Leave
Maternity leave is 14 weeks of paid leave. Paternity leave is 3 paid days, to be taken within one month of the birth.
