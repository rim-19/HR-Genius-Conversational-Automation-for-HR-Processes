# Employee Benefits — HR-Genius Technologies

## Health Insurance
All permanent employees receive private health insurance from their first day, covering the employee and their immediate dependents. Coverage includes consultations, hospitalization, and prescription medication.

## Professional Development
Each employee has an annual **learning budget of 5,000 MAD** for courses, certifications, and conferences relevant to their role. Requests are approved by the direct manager. HR-Genius Technologies also sponsors one internal "Learning Day" per quarter.

## Meal & Transport
Employees receive meal vouchers for each in-office day and a monthly transport allowance. Remote days are not eligible for meal vouchers.

## Wellbeing
The company offers an Employee Assistance Programme with confidential counselling, plus subsidized gym membership. Two "wellbeing days" per year may be taken with no reason required, separate from annual and sick leave.

## Referral Bonus
Employees who refer a candidate that is hired and passes probation receive a referral bonus of 6,000 MAD.
