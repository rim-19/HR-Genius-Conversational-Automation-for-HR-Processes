# Probation & Performance — HR-Genius Technologies

## Probation Period
New employees serve a **probation period of 3 months**. During probation, either party may end the employment with 7 days' notice. A probation review meeting is held before the end of the period to confirm the permanent role.

## Performance Reviews
Formal performance reviews occur **twice a year** (June and December). Reviews assess objectives, competencies, and development goals agreed with the manager. Outcomes inform salary reviews and promotions.

## Promotions
Promotions are based on sustained performance, readiness for greater responsibility, and business need. A promotion is documented with an official letter issued by the Human Resources Department and may include a salary adjustment.

## Notice Periods
After probation, the standard notice period is **1 month** for most roles and **2 months** for senior and management roles, unless the employment contract states otherwise.

## Resignation
Resignations should be submitted in writing to the direct manager and the Human Resources Department. HR will arrange an exit interview and return of company equipment.
