# IT & Security Policy — HR-Genius Technologies

## Accounts & Passwords
Every employee receives company accounts on their first day. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are rotated every 6 months. Multi-factor authentication is required for all company systems. Never share credentials.

## Acceptable Use
Company devices and accounts are for work purposes. Limited personal use is tolerated but must not interfere with duties or breach the law. Installing unapproved software is not permitted.

## Data Protection
HR-Genius Technologies handles personal data in line with applicable data-protection law. Employee and client data must only be accessed on a need-to-know basis. Data must not be copied to personal devices or unapproved cloud services.

## Device Security
Laptops must have disk encryption and an auto-lock of 5 minutes enabled. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT immediately. Company data on personal phones must be protected by a passcode.

## Incident Reporting
Suspected security incidents, phishing, or data breaches must be reported to the IT team within 24 hours through the HR-Genius platform or the IT helpdesk.
