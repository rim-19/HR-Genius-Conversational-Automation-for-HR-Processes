# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy — HR-Genius Technologies

## Hybrid Model
HR-Genius Technologies operates a **hybrid work model**. Employees are expected to work from the Casablanca office at **12 Innovation Avenue** for a minimum of **3 days per week**, with up to 2 days of remote work permitted. Teams may agree on fixed in-office "anchor days" to support collaboration.

## Full Remote
Fully remote arrangements are considered case by case and require approval from the department head and the Human Resources Department. They are reviewed every 6 months.

## Expectations
Remote employees must be reachable during **core hours (10:00–16:00)**, keep their calendar up to date, and use company-approved tools for communication and file sharing. Home internet and a suitable workspace are the employee's responsibility; a one-time home-office stipend of 2,000 MAD is available on request.

## Equipment
Company laptops may be used at home. Equipment remains the property of HR-Genius Technologies and must be returned upon leaving the company.
