# Code of Conduct — HR-Genius Technologies

## Our Standards
Everyone at HR-Genius Technologies is expected to act with integrity, respect, and professionalism. We are committed to an inclusive workplace free of discrimination and harassment of any kind, based on gender, religion, origin, disability, or any other protected characteristic.

## Anti-Harassment
Harassment, bullying, and retaliation are strictly prohibited. Concerns can be raised confidentially with the Human Resources Department or through the anonymous reporting channel on the HR-Genius platform. All reports are investigated promptly and treated confidentially.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or family interest that could conflict with their duties. Accepting gifts of significant value from clients or suppliers is not permitted.

## Confidentiality
Company, client, and employee information must be kept confidential and used only for legitimate business purposes, during and after employment.

## Disciplinary Process
Breaches of this code are handled through a progressive process: informal discussion, written warning, final written warning, and, for serious cases, termination. Serious misconduct may lead to immediate dismissal.
